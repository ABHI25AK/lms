How FIFO Reservations Work

The Library Management System strictly operates on a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) queue for fair access:

  • When a checked-out book is returned by a borrower, the system locks that physical copy and alerts the student at Queue Position #1.
  • A hold is placed on the copy for {{ library_settings.reservation_pickup_hours }} hours.
  • If the student fails to pick up the book before the deadline, the reservation automatically expires and the copy is transferred to the next waiting student in line.
